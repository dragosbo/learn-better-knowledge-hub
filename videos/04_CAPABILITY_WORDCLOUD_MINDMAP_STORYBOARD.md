# Video Storyboard: Capability 04 — Word Cloud & Mind Map Hub
## 5-Scene Script, Technical Cues & Production Blueprint

- **Capability**: 04 — Word Cloud & Mind Map Hub
- **Module ID**: `cap-04`
- **Estimated Runtime**: 4 Minutes 40 Seconds
- **Voiceover**: Professional, educational, senior engineering architect tone (Web Speech Synthesis / NotebookLM Audio)
- **Target Audience**: Software Engineers, AI Researchers, and Self-Directed Learners

---

### Scene 1: The Curse of Lexical Blindness in Video Libraries (00:00 - 00:50)
- **Visual Setup**:
  - Split screen: On the left, a traditional YouTube search bar showing 0 results when searching for conceptual terms ("synthetic cognition"); on the right, the Learn Better playlist manager with 71 playlists.
  - Red flashing warning tag: *"Lexical Blindness: When you don't know the exact keyword to search for."*
- **On-Screen Text**: *"Beyond Keyword Search: Extracting Semantic Density from Video Curricula"*
- **Camera & Animation Motion**:
  - Slow zoom into a dense text corpus of video titles, descriptions, and notes. Words scramble and dissolve into raw token counts.
- **Narrator Voiceover Script**:
  > *"When you study technical subjects through video, you inevitably hit the wall of lexical blindness. YouTube search only works if you already know the exact phrasing the creator used in their title. A student searching for 'reasoning algorithms' will easily miss a masterclass on 'test-time compute' or 'MCTS tree search'. In this fourth capability of the Learn Better series, we explore how semantic NLP transforms raw video metadata into an interactive concept word cloud and a synchronized three-tier mind map—giving you instant topological clarity over what you are about to learn."*

---

### Scene 2: The Top 50 Concept Cloud & Tokenization Pipeline (00:50 - 01:55)
- **Visual Setup**:
  - Live animated bubble cloud: 50 vibrant concept capsules floating with fluid physics, sized proportionally to their frequency (from 'Intelligence' weight 248 down to 'Synthesis' weight 29).
  - Domain color legend across the top: Blue for *Core Domain*, Indigo for *Architecture*, Emerald for *Algorithms*, Amber for *Hardware*, Purple for *NLP & LLMs*, Rose for *Economics*.
  - An algorithmic overlay sliding in from the right demonstrating the tokenization pipeline: raw text corpus -> lowercase normalization -> stopword pruning -> unigram/bigram frequency scoring.
- **On-Screen Text**: *"42,680 Tokens Ingested • 3,840 Unique Vocabulary Words • Top 50 Frequency Scored"*
- **Camera & Animation Motion**:
  - Cursor clicks on category pill `Hardware`. The cloud dynamically filters, fading out unrelated tokens and highlighting `Groq`, `TPU`, `GPU`, `Silicon`, `Memory`, and `Compute`.
- **Narrator Voiceover Script**:
  > *"At the core of Capability 04 is an automated tokenization pipeline. In our flagship Intelligence curriculum, the system ingests over 42,000 text tokens across twelve lectures. It strips syntactic noise and video platform boilerplate like 'subscribe' and 'watch', calculating relative frequency weights for every technical term. The result is this interactive Top-50 Word Cloud. You can filter by category with a single click—isolating hardware constraints like Groq LPUs and memory bandwidth walls from mathematical optimization algorithms like AdamW and SwiGLU."*

### Scene 3: The 3-Tier Hierarchical Mind Map Architecture (01:55 - 03:00)
- **Visual Setup**:
  - Smooth transition to the 3-Tier Mind Map view.
  - Tier 1 (Center/Root): Glowing indigo node labeled *Intelligence: Biological to Synthetic*.
  - Tier 2 (Pillars): 5 radiating structural branches: *Origins & Biology*, *Neural Mathematics*, *LLM Architectures & Agents*, *Hardware Silicon*, and *Macro-Economics*.
  - Tier 3 (Leaf Nodes): Collapsible video cards branching out from each pillar with channel badges, playtime chips, and synthesized takeaway bullet points.
- **On-Screen Text**: *"3-Tier Cognitive Taxonomy: Root Thesis ➔ Conceptual Pillars ➔ Concrete Video Lectures"*
- **Camera & Animation Motion**:
  - Expanding the *Origins & Biology* branch to reveal Manolis Kellis's lecture on polygenic genetics and The Symbiont's chapter on cellular chemotaxis.
- **Narrator Voiceover Script**:
  > *"While a word cloud reveals what concepts dominate a curriculum, it cannot explain how those concepts relate. For that, we switch to the Three-Tier Mind Map. At Tier One sits the root learning objective. Tier Two branches into five foundational pillars. And at Tier Three, concrete video lectures are anchored directly to their conceptual parents. Instead of wondering why a lecture on evolutionary biology belongs in an AI playlist, the mind map explicitly diagrams how cellular feedback loops provided the evolutionary blueprint for modern reinforcement learning."*
